How DriveVault Works

Three steps to never lose a file again.

1

Scan your drives

Plug in a drive and hit scan - that's it. DriveVault indexes every file, folder, and piece of metadata in minutes. Choose Full Scan for thumbnail previews you can browse offline, or Quick Scan to catalogue massive drives in seconds. Once scanned, you'll never need to guess what's on that drive again.

2

Search everything, unplugged

Need that client project from 2019? Type a few letters and DriveVault finds it across every drive you've ever scanned - even the ones sitting in a drawer. Search by name, path, file type, or metadata. No cables required.

3

Organize, compare, and share

Tag files, rate your best shots, and add notes without touching the original drive. Compare two drives to verify backups or spot missing files. Export your entire library and share it with a teammate - they'll see everything you see, instantly. Professional tools, zero learning curve.

See your files without the drive

DriveVault generates low-res visual previews of every image and video - so you can browse your archives like they're right in front of you. It's like having your entire hard drive in a tiny fraction of the space. Even when the drive is on a shelf, in storage, or across the world.

One scan. Permanent access.

Plug in a drive, hit scan, and DriveVault captures everything - file names, folder structures, metadata, and thumbnail previews for images and videos. Use Full Scan for visual browsing with rich metadata, or Quick Scan to catalogue even massive drives in seconds. Your files stay private. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Find any file in seconds - across every drive

Looking for that shoot from three years ago? Type a few letters in the menu bar and DriveVault surfaces it instantly - no need to open the app, no need to plug anything in. Filter by file type, date, size, or metadata to zero in on exactly what you need.

Know your backups actually worked

Select two drives and DriveVault instantly compares them - file by file. See exactly what's missing from the smaller drive and where those files live on the larger one. No more hoping your backup is complete. Get a detailed report that shows the truth.

Group drives the way your brain works

Drag drives into Projects and organise them however makes sense to you - by client, by year, by shoot, by vibe. When you've got dozens of drives, structure is everything. DriveVault makes it effortless.

See the big picture

How much storage do you actually have? Where is it all going? Library Stats breaks down your entire archive - total capacity, space used, file types, and more. Finally understand what you're working with across every drive you own.

Share your archive with your team

Export your library - or just specific drives - and send it to a colleague. They'll see everything you see: folder structures, previews, metadata, search. One person scans. Everyone benefits. No cloud required.
